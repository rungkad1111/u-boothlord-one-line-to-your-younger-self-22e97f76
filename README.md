## Entry

> **Don't sell the bike to impress the girl who never rides — you'll spend your thirties buying back the summers you sold for nothing.**

Length: 130 characters (gate: 1–200 characters).

## Why this line

A single line an older self would actually send back: one sentence, no explanation, no second sentence doing the work. It is drawn from a life actually lived — a specific bike, a specific girl who never rides, a specific decade spent paying for the trade — not a motto anyone could wear. The turn lands in one breath: the summers sold for nothing, then bought back at a higher price in your thirties. Honest and specific enough to sting a little, hard-won and warm rather than sentimental.
